*Claimed* = at least one rule is tagged with the technique (family match); *measured* = such a rule fired on-target on a recording of it. Technique coverage counts partially detected techniques as covered; *fully detected* requires every recording of the technique to be detected. Brackets are 95% Wilson intervals computed from the raw counts and rounded once.

Measured is a subset of claimed by construction (a technique can only be measured by a rule tagged with it), so every discordant technique points the same way and a McNemar test would only restate their count. The overstatement is therefore reported as claimed-but-not-measured techniques / techniques, in points, with a Wilson interval. *Gaps*: a tagged rule's (channel, EventID) target never occurs in a recording of the technique (telemetry) or does occur but no tagged rule matched (rule logic).
